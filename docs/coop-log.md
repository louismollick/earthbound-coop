### Simultaneous battle input (item 1): claimed menus
- Design: first come, first served (real parallel menus don't fit one mode stack, global menu state and a 256 px screen). A turn's command menus open in the order Players press A for them. A press while another Player's menu is open queues for the next. A claim opens the claimer's first unchosen Hero, so a Player's own Heroes keep vanilla order. When only one Player has Heroes left, or during Auto Fight, menus open by themselves in Party order. Initiative still decides who acts first. B goes back to the same Player's previous Hero (or reopens their first), never to another Player's choice. Run Away and Auto Fight stay on the first menu of the turn, now the first one claimed.
- Code: `src/coop/coop_battle.c` (`coop_battle_claim`, `coop_battle_note_claims`, `coop_battle_back`, the cue). Hooks: `btl_start_turn` (existing line, now also starts claiming); `btl_step_menu_body` hands over to `btl_step_menu_body_coop` (battle.c, ~40 lines, it needs the static skip/store helpers) while `coop_battle_claiming()`; `btl_step_menu_decide` routes B to `coop_battle_back`; `coop_input_feed` queues claims. Gated on co-op: claiming is only set by the co-op `btl_start_turn` hook.
- Cue: while the battle waits for a claim, the PSI-name box (window 0x26) at the top shows who may claim ("P1  P2", up to "P1 P2 P3 P4"); it closes when a menu opens. Harness: PLAYERS ends `| choose=P1,P2` during the wait and `until input` is met there; `lib.State.choose` and `Session.command_menu()` (advance, then claim) added.
